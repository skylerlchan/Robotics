# Bobyard -- Comprehensive Company Research

**Date:** April 20, 2026

## Company Overview

Bobyard is an AI-powered construction technology platform that automates takeoffs and estimating for contractors. The company uses proprietary computer vision (CV) and natural language processing (NLP) models to interpret construction blueprints and generate quantity/material takeoffs -- the most time-consuming step in preconstruction. Their tagline is "powering how the world gets built" ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251210946193/en/Bobyard-Raises-\$35-Million-Series-A-to-Power-How-the-World-Gets-Built)).

* **Legal Name:** Bobyard, Inc.
* **Founded:** 2023
* **Headquarters:** San Francisco, CA (Crunchbase lists Menlo Park; Tracxn lists Cupertino -- the company's press release says San Francisco) ([Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/bobyard), [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251210946193/en/Bobyard-Raises-\$35-Million-Series-A-to-Power-How-the-World-Gets-Built))

## Product & Technology

### What Bobyard Does

Bobyard automates the construction **takeoff** process -- the step where estimators measure and count materials needed from construction drawings/blueprints. This is the foundation of all preconstruction: every estimate, schedule, and procurement decision begins with a takeoff ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com/about)).

The platform turns hours of manual work into minutes of accurate output, making cost estimates up to **10x faster** while eliminating costly mistakes ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com/about)).

### How It Works

1. Contractors **upload a PDF** of construction drawings
2. They **select a legend** on the drawings
3. Run the **AI analysis**
4. Receive **takeoff quantities in seconds**

Under the hood, the platform runs **dozens of proprietary computer vision models simultaneously** -- symbol counting, linear measurement, area detection, text parsing -- each built from scratch and optimized for construction drawings ([8VC](https://www.8vc.com/resources/from-plans-to-progress-announcing-the-8vc-bobyard-partnership)).

### Core Features

* **Auto Count:** Instantly detect and count planting, irrigation, and electrical symbols ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com))
* **Auto Area Measurement:** Automatically measure pavers, concrete, and other materials ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com))
* **Auto Length Measurement:** Measure beds, edges, and hardscape in seconds ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com))
* **Pattern Search:** Automatically generates precise polygons and area totals for hatch patterns like sod, pavers, mulch, or pavement ([Landscape Management](https://www.landscapemanagement.net/bobyard-releases-a-new-ai-tool-for-landscapers/))
* **Custom Templates:** Adaptable formulas to fit specific business workflows ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com))
* **Database & Vendor Quotes:** Track historical costs, vendor quotes, assemblies ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com))
* **Flexible Exports:** Clean estimates to PDF or Excel ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com))
* **AI-powered Q\&A:** Trained on user data ([Startup Seeker](https://startup-seeker.com/company/bobyard~com))

## Market Focus

### Current Focus: Commercial Landscaping

Bobyard started with **commercial landscaping** -- deliberately chosen as one of the most complex and overlooked trades in construction ([8VC](https://www.8vc.com/resources/from-plans-to-progress-announcing-the-8vc-bobyard-partnership)). Landscaping drawings are dense and non-standard: planting sheets can include thousands of shrubs across dozens of species, with arbitrary symbols that vary from project to project.

**Target customers:** Mid-market and enterprise landscape contractors handling commercial installation projects valued between **$3M and $20M+**. These contractors manage dozens of bids per month ([Landscape Management](https://www.landscapemanagement.net/bobyard-releases-a-new-ai-tool-for-landscapers/)).

### Expansion Roadmap

With the Series A funding, Bobyard is expanding into additional trades including ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251210946193/en/Bobyard-Raises-\$35-Million-Series-A-to-Power-How-the-World-Gets-Built)):

* **Drywall**
* **Electrical**
* **HVAC**
* **Plumbing**
* **Framing**

8VC compared this playbook to their other investment Bedrock: "win the hardest problem first, then use that technical and operational edge to scale across verticals" ([8VC](https://www.8vc.com/resources/from-plans-to-progress-announcing-the-8vc-bobyard-partnership)).

### Market Size

Construction, landscaping, and the trades represent more than **\$1T in annual U.S. spend**, yet remain among the least digitized industries ([Pear VC](https://pear.vc/bobyard-series-a/)).

## Industry Context

### The Problem Bobyard Solves

US construction labor productivity has **flatlined since 1950 while other industries have nearly tripled** ([8VC](https://www.8vc.com/resources/from-plans-to-progress-announcing-the-8vc-bobyard-partnership)). Construction is one of the largest industries in the world but one of the least technologically advanced.

The takeoff and estimating workflow is a bottleneck:

* Estimators manually place color-coded dots on each item, trace polygons for sod/mulch/pavers, measure irrigation lines, and plug it all into spreadsheets
* This process takes **hours to days** per project
* It limits bid volume and caps growth
* For smaller firms, it means stretched employees; for larger firms, it caps bid volume ([8VC](https://www.8vc.com/resources/from-plans-to-progress-announcing-the-8vc-bobyard-partnership))

### Why Now

Recent advances in GenAI and CV/NLP have made it possible to build models that interpret construction drawings with the intuition of experienced estimators. This level of automation simply was not possible before ([Pear VC](https://pear.vc/bobyard-series-a/)).

## Important Clarification: Bobyard is NOT a Robotics Company

Despite the initial search premise, Bobyard is **not** a robotics company. It is an **AI/SaaS construction technology company** focused on software -- specifically computer vision and NLP models for automating construction takeoffs and estimating from blueprints. There is no hardware, robotics, or physical automation component. The company's "construction" focus is in preconstruction estimating software, not physical construction robotics.
